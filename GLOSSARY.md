# Badminque

A club-first badminton rating and match-tracking system delivering universal skill transparency for recreational and competitive players.

## Language

**Club Night**:
A single session where badminton players assemble at a venue to play matches.
_Avoid_: Event, meetup, tournament

**Organizer**:
The club member administering the club night session and recording match scores on the tablet.
_Avoid_: Admin, host, manager

**Roster**:
The collection of players checked in to participate in a given club night session.
_Avoid_: Guest list, sign-up sheet, attendee list

**Match**:
A scored contest between two sides (singles or doubles) comprising one or more games.
_Avoid_: Game, bout, fixture

**Rating**:
The numerical calculation representing a player's relative skill level, updated from match results.
_Avoid_: Score, rank, level, handicap

**Roster Player**:
A player entry recorded on a club roster by an organizer who does not yet have an individual login.
_Avoid_: Unregistered player, guest, phantom user

**Claimed Player**:
A player who has registered an account and linked their identity to their past match history and roster entries.
_Avoid_: Verified user, active account, linked member
